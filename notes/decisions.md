- 2026-11-01: Delivery compares each pitch's mean release point with his primary fastball's, the most-used FF/SI/FC. Release positions are in inches, and arm angle uses Statcast's arm_angle.
- 2026-11-01: Tunneling compares back-to-back pitches of different types, thrown by the same pitcher in the same PA. Positions at 23.8 ft (and 20 and 25 ft) use Statcast's 9-parameter fit, with the start point back-solved from plate_x/plate_z. Distances are measured from the plate in Statcast's y coordinate. A pair "tunnels" when its distance at 23.8 ft is below the league median for that pitch pair and hand. The break-to-tunnel ratio caps the tunnel distance at a 0.5-inch minimum to avoid dividing by near zero.
- 2026-11-01: First full run of Step 5A: 140,541 league relief pitch pairs, 5,060 of them KC. League medians across reliever pitch pairs are about 9.5 to 10.5 inches apart at 23.8 ft and 17 to 21 inches at the plate. Per-pair whiff splits rest on 25 to 80 pairs, so treat them as directional.
- 2026-11-01: Step 5 credits each PA to the pitcher of its last pitch. Runner-out and other non-batter events (caught stealing, pickoffs, wild pitches) don't count as PAs. 3PS% = two strikes reached within the first three pitches (the 4th pitch or earlier thrown at 2 strikes), or an out within three pitches. Kill% = K / PAs that reached a two-strike count. Count states: two strikes first, then ahead (strikes > balls), even and behind.
